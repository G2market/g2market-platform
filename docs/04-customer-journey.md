# G2Market Customer Journey

## Step 1: Customer Signup

- Customer creates an account
- Email verification is completed

---

## Step 2: Virtual Address Generation

- System generates a unique customer ID
- Virtual Indian address is assigned

Example:

John Smith

G2M-10001

I-402, Bharti Park H-I CHS,
Mira Road East,
Thane, Maharashtra,
India - 401107

---

## Step 3: Customer Shops Online

- Customer shops from Amazon India, Flipkart or other stores
- Customer uses the G2Market address

---

## Step 4: Warehouse Receives Package

- Warehouse staff receives the package
- Photos are uploaded
- Weight and dimensions are recorded

---

## Step 5: Customer Notification

- Customer receives an email notification
- Package appears in the dashboard

---

## Step 6: Shipment Request

- Customer selects packages
- Customer chooses shipping method

---

## Step 7: Payment

- Customer pays the shipping charges

---

## Step 8: Dispatch

- Shipment is created
- Tracking number is generated

---

## Step 9: Delivery

- Package is delivered to the customer
